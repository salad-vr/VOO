Chapter Letter | September 2024
By the President

Welcome to a New Year at VOO

Dear Brothers and Friends of VOO,

As the autumn term begins, the Lodge once again fills with the energy of returning members and the curiosity of new students discovering VOO for the first time. This year promises to be one of renewal and deepening tradition.

We have an ambitious calendar of events planned, from our traditional Wine & Poetry evenings to a symposium on technology and human connection. The Lodge has received fresh paint and repairs thanks to the summer work of several dedicated members, and we are grateful to the alumni whose support made this possible.

To our alumni: we hope to see many of you at the Alumni Reunion in November. The active chapter is eager to hear your stories and share what VOO has become in your absence. To prospective members: we invite you to attend an open event and discover what makes this chapter unique.

Yours in the bond,

[President Name]
President, VOO 2024–2025
