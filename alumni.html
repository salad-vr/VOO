Alumni

Welcome Back to VOO

The VOO brotherhood extends far beyond graduation. Alumni remain an essential part of chapter life, bringing experience, perspective, and enduring friendship to the Lodge.

Homecoming

Once a Member, Always a Brother

Whether you graduated last year or fifty years ago, the Lodge is your home. VOO alumni are doctors, lawyers, engineers, teachers, artists, entrepreneurs, and public servants. What unites them is the shared experience of chapter life and the enduring bonds formed at the University of Western Ontario.

We invite you to return for events, share your experience with current members, update your information so we can keep you informed, and consider supporting the chapter that helped shape your university years.

Alumni Events

Gatherings designed to reconnect alumni with the chapter and one another

🎉

Alumni Reunion

Our annual reunion brings together alumni from across decades for dinner, toasts, and the rekindling of old friendships. A chance to see the Lodge, meet current members, and share stories.

🍽️

Formal Dinners

Quarterly formal dinners at the Lodge exclusively for alumni and invited guests. Features the traditions, ceremonies, and toasts that defined your years at VOO.

🏠

Lodge Gatherings

Informal evenings for alumni to visit the Lodge, meet current members, and see how the chapter has evolved while honoring its traditions.

🎓

Guest Talks & Symposia

Alumni are regularly invited to present on their professional expertise, life experiences, or research, contributing to the chapter's ongoing intellectual life.

Giving Back

Mentorship Program

Alumni mentorship connects experienced professionals with current members navigating academic and career decisions. The relationships formed through this program often extend far beyond graduation.

  • ✦ Career Conversations: One-on-one discussions about career paths, industry insights, and professional development.
  • ✦ Graduate School Guidance: Advice on advanced degrees, research programs, and academic careers from those who have pursued them.
  • ✦ Chapter History & Continuity: Alumni who share stories of VOO in years past help current members understand the chapter's legacy.

In Remembrance

Honoring brothers who have passed

We remember our brothers who have left us, grateful for their friendship, their contributions to VOO, and the example they set for those who followed. Their memory lives in the traditions they upheld and the lives they touched.

— VOO Memorial Tradition

If you would like to share a remembrance of a brother who has passed, or learn more about our memorial practices, please contact the Alumni Chair or use our general contact form.
