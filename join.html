A Chapter for the Curious

VOO seeks students who value conversation, friendship, and tradition. If you are drawn to intellectual community and genuine brotherhood, we invite you to learn more.

Who Should Reach Out

VOO is selective but welcoming. We look for students who share our commitments.

📚

Students of Ideas

Students interested in discussion, literature, philosophy, debate, history, arts, and the life of the mind. You need not be a literature major; you need only be genuinely curious.

🤝

Students of Character

Students looking for chapter life beyond a standard social calendar. Those who value accountability, mentorship, and friendships that demand something of them.

🏛️

Students of Tradition

Students who want to contribute to a long-standing University of Western Ontario tradition. Those who understand that institutions are sustained by the care of their members.

The Rush Process

What to Expect

Our rush process is designed to help you understand VOO and help us understand you. It is not an interrogation; it is a mutual exploration of fit and shared values.

  • ✦ Conversations with Members: Informal meetings with active brothers to learn about chapter life and ask questions.
  • ✦ Open Events: Attend Wine & Poetry, Open Philosophicals, or other public events to see VOO in action.
  • ✦ Learn Our Culture: Understanding VOO traditions, expectations, and the commitments of membership.
  • ✦ Respectful Questions Both Ways: We ask about your interests and character; you ask about ours. Honesty on both sides is essential.
🌿

"Rush is not about selling VOO. It is about discovering whether VOO is right for you."

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from prospective members

Do I need to study literature to join? +

Absolutely not. VOO members come from every faculty and program at the University of Western Ontario. What matters is genuine curiosity and a willingness to engage with ideas. Engineers, scientists, philosophers, and artists have all found a home at VOO.

Can I attend an event before expressing interest? +

Yes. Many of our events, particularly Open Philosophicals and Wine & Poetry evenings, are open to invited guests. Attending an event is an excellent way to understand VOO culture before deciding whether to pursue membership.

Is VOO only social? +

VOO is not merely a social organization, though we do enjoy one another's company. We are a literary fraternity committed to intellectual life, character development, and lasting brotherhood. Social events serve our larger purposes; they are not ends in themselves.

How does rush work? +

Rush involves a series of conversations, events, and mutual assessments over several weeks. There is no single interview or test. The process is designed to give both you and the chapter time to determine whether VOO is the right fit.

Who can I contact with private questions? +

You may reach out to our Rush Chair directly, or use the general contact form on our Contact page. All inquiries are treated with discretion and respect. No question is too small or too tentative.

Still Have Questions?

We welcome your curiosity. Reach out through our contact form or attend an open event to learn more about VOO in person.
