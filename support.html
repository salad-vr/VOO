Support VOO

Help Carry VOO Forward

Support helps preserve the Lodge, strengthen chapter programming, welcome alumni back into chapter life, maintain archives, and keep the chapter accessible for future members.

What Your Support Funds

Every contribution sustains a tradition that has enriched the University of Western Ontario since 1948

🏠

Lodge Upkeep

The Lodge is the chapter home and a physical link to our history. Support helps with repairs, maintenance, utilities, and improvements that keep the Lodge safe, welcoming, and functional for current and future members.

📚

Chapter Programming

From symposia and poetry nights to formal dinners and rush events, chapter programming requires resources. Support helps VOO offer rich, consistent programming without placing undue burden on student members.

🤝

Alumni Events

Reunions, formal dinners, and Lodge gatherings for alumni strengthen the network that makes VOO special. Support helps make these events accessible and memorable for alumni across generations.

📜

Chapter Archives

VOO's history is preserved in photographs, letters, programs, and artifacts. Support helps digitize, organize, and protect these materials so the chapter's memory is not lost to time.

💚

Member Accessibility

Not every member can afford dues, event costs, or formal attire without assistance. Support helps ensure that financial constraints never prevent a deserving student from fully participating in chapter life.

⏳

Long-Term Continuity

VOO has survived for decades because past members invested in its future. Support builds the financial and organizational foundation that allows the chapter to weather challenges and continue for generations to come.

Accountability and Transparency

VOO is committed to using all support responsibly and transparently. Supporters receive regular updates on how their contributions are used, including:

  • ✦ Annual reports on Lodge condition and improvement projects
  • ✦ Updates on chapter programming and events funded by support
  • ✦ Recognition in Chapter Letters and at appropriate events (with permission)
  • ✦ Access to alumni events and Lodge visits for major supporters

If you have questions about how support is used or wish to designate your contribution for a specific purpose, please contact the Treasurer or use our general contact form.
