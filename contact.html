Frequently Asked Questions

Questions submitted by visitors, answered for the benefit of all

What does VOO stand for? +

VOO is the local chapter designation of the Kappa Alpha Literary Society at the University of Western Ontario. Like many chapter designations in literary and debating societies, it carries historical significance specific to the chapter's founding and traditions.

Can parents or family members attend events? +

Some events, such as the Christmas Formal and certain alumni gatherings, welcome family members. Other events are restricted to members and invited guests. Please contact the Events Chair for specific information about upcoming occasions.

How can I support VOO without giving money? +

There are many ways to support VOO beyond financial contributions. Alumni can mentor current members, offer career advice, attend events, help with Lodge work days, or contribute their expertise to chapter programming. Community members can spread the word about VOO's activities and mission.

Is VOO affiliated with the University of Western Ontario officially? +

VOO is a student organization at the University of Western Ontario, operating within the university's framework for clubs and societies. We maintain strong relationships with university administration while preserving our independent traditions and governance as a chapter of the Kappa Alpha Literary Society.

How do I visit the Lodge? +

The Lodge is a private chapter home, not generally open to the public. Alumni, prospective members, and invited guests may visit by arrangement with the Lodge Steward or appropriate officer. Contact us to inquire about visiting.
