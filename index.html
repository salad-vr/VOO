What Is VOO?

A Chapter Built Around Thoughtful Company

VOO is the local University of Western Ontario chapter of the Kappa Alpha Literary Society. Founded at the University of Western Ontario in 1948, the chapter carries forward a literary and debating tradition through conversation, philosophy, poetry, formal gatherings, alumni connection, and everyday life at the Lodge.

This is a chapter built around thoughtful company. Members come together for discussion, friendship, events, service, and the shared responsibility of keeping a long-standing University of Western Ontario tradition alive.

The Life of VOO

Three pillars define our chapter at the University of Western Ontario

✦

Literary Society

From poetry nights and open philosophicals to symposia and formal discussion, VOO keeps conversation at the centre of chapter life. We believe that ideas improve when they are shared, questioned, and refined in good company.

Explore Our Traditions
◈

Brotherhood

VOO is a close chapter of students and alumni who value friendship, accountability, mentorship, and the habits of good conversation. Our brotherhood extends far beyond graduation, creating lifelong connections across generations.

Meet the Chapter
⌂

The Lodge

The Lodge is the chapter home: a place for meetings, dinners, alumni gatherings, late conversations, and the continuity of VOO at the University of Western Ontario. It is both a physical space and a symbol of the chapter's enduring presence.

Help Carry VOO Forward

VOO depends on the care of its members, alumni, and supporters. Support helps preserve the Lodge, strengthen chapter programming, welcome alumni back into chapter life, maintain archives, and keep the chapter accessible for future members.

Every contribution, whether financial or through mentorship and presence, sustains a tradition that has enriched the University of Western Ontario since 1948.
