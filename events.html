Recurring Events

The regular gatherings that structure chapter life

🍷

Wine & Poetry

Monthly evenings of readings and conversation. Members and guests share favorite poems, original work, and reflections on literature.

💭

Open Philosophicals

Bi-weekly moderated discussions on questions of ethics, politics, metaphysics, and daily life. Open to members and invited guests.

🎓

Symposia

Formal evenings of prepared presentations and discussion. Members research and present on topics of shared interest.

🍽️

Formal Dinners

Regular formal dinners at the Lodge, featuring toasts, readings, and the ceremonies that have structured VOO meals for decades.

🤝

Philanthropy Events

Service projects and fundraising events that connect VOO to the broader London community and causes members care about.

🎭

Rush Events

Open gatherings during rush season where prospective members meet the chapter, learn about traditions, and experience VOO hospitality.

Signature Events

The annual occasions that mark the VOO calendar

Annual Tradition

Masquerade Ball

Our most formal social occasion, held each year with costume, music, and the traditions that make it one of the most anticipated events on the chapter calendar. A night of elegance and mystery at the Lodge.

🎭
December

Christmas Formal

An evening of dinner, toasts, and celebration of the year past. Alumni often join the active chapter for this warm conclusion to the autumn term, sharing memories and looking ahead to the new year.

🎄
November

Alumni Reunion

A dedicated gathering for alumni and the active chapter to reconnect, share stories from their years at VOO, and look ahead to the chapter's future. Often includes Lodge tours and a formal dinner.

🤝
September

Harvest Gala

A fall celebration that brings together members, alumni, and guests to mark the beginning of the academic year. Features seasonal food, readings, and the induction of new members into chapter traditions.
