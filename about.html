About VOO

The Kappa Alpha Literary Society at Western University

A chapter rooted in conversation, open discussion, brotherhood, alumni tradition, and life at the Lodge since 1948.

Our Identity

VOO in London, Ontario

VOO is the Western University chapter of the Kappa Alpha Literary Society, locally known simply as VOO. We are a literary fraternity in the truest sense: a society of students and alumni bound together by a shared commitment to conversation, intellectual curiosity, and brotherly friendship.

Our chapter is small by design. We believe that meaningful connection requires intimacy, that ideas improve through challenge, and that character is refined through sustained relationship. Every member of VOO is both a student and a teacher, learning from one another in formal symposia, informal gatherings at the Lodge, and the ongoing conversation that defines chapter life.

We are not a social club, though we enjoy one another's company immensely. We are not an academic society, though we take ideas seriously. We are a fraternity in the original sense of the word: a brotherhood of shared purpose and mutual care.

Chapter Culture

The habits and traditions that define life at VOO

🗣️

Open Discussion

Every member is encouraged to speak, question, and disagree respectfully. Our conversations range across philosophy, politics, literature, science, and the practical questions of young adulthood. No topic is forbidden if approached with sincerity and goodwill.

📖

Literary & Debating Roots

From our founding, VOO has maintained the tradition of literary discussion and formal debate. We host regular symposia, poetry readings, and philosophical inquiries that challenge members to articulate and defend their convictions.

🤝

Brotherhood & Mentorship

Older members mentor newer ones, not through formal programs but through sustained presence. Alumni remain deeply connected to the active chapter, offering guidance, friendship, and the perspective that comes with experience.

🍷

Formal Gatherings

Formal dinners, wine and poetry evenings, and ceremonial occasions punctuate the academic year. These events remind us that intellectual life is not separate from aesthetic and social refinement.

🏠

Life at the Lodge

The Lodge is our chapter home and the physical center of VOO life. It is where meetings are held, meals are shared, alumni return, and the chapter's memory is preserved in photographs, books, and artifacts.

🌟

Alumni Tradition

Our alumni network spans decades and professions. Regular reunions, mentorship connections, and the ongoing life of the Lodge ensure that the VOO brotherhood extends far beyond graduation.

Our History

From literary society origins to present-day chapter life at Western

1825

Kappa Alpha Founded

The Kappa Alpha Society was founded as a literary society, establishing traditions of debate, discussion, and brotherly friendship that would spread to colleges and universities across North America.

1948

VOO Founded at Western

The VOO chapter was established at Western University in London, Ontario, bringing the Kappa Alpha tradition of literary fellowship to the campus. The chapter quickly established its own character, blending national traditions with local customs.

Today

Chapter Life Continues

VOO continues through discussion, events, alumni ties, and Lodge life. Today's members uphold the same commitment to conversation and brotherhood that defined the chapter in 1948, adapting traditions to the needs of each new generation.
