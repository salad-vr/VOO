How to Reach Us

For general questions, use the Contact / Questions page, which will direct your inquiry to the appropriate officer. For specific questions about membership, reach out to the Rush Chair. For alumni matters, contact the Alumni Chair. For financial or support questions, the Treasurer is your best point of contact.
