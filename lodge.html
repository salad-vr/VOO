The Lodge

The Chapter Home of VOO

The Lodge is more than a building. It is the physical center of chapter life, the keeper of memory, and the responsibility of every generation of VOO members.

Daily Life

Chapter Life at the Lodge

The Lodge is where VOO lives. It is where members gather for meetings, share meals, study together, and build the friendships that define their university years. The walls hold decades of conversation, debate, laughter, and the quiet moments of companionship that make a fraternity truly fraternal.

  • ✦ Meetings: Regular chapter meetings, officer sessions, and planning gatherings.
  • ✦ Informal Conversation: Late-night discussions, study sessions, and spontaneous gatherings.
  • ✦ Formal Gatherings: Ceremonial occasions, formal dinners, and chapter traditions.
  • ✦ Shared Meals: Regular dinners that bring members together around the table.
🏠

"The Lodge is where VOO becomes real."

Events at the Lodge

The Lodge hosts gatherings that define VOO's calendar

🍷

Poetry Nights

Wine & Poetry evenings transform the Lodge into a salon of literature and conversation, with readings by members and invited guests.

👴

Alumni Evenings

When alumni return to the Lodge, the conversation bridges generations. Stories of VOO past meet the energy of VOO present.

🎓

Symposia

Formal evenings of prepared presentations and moderated discussion, continuing the literary society tradition that founded VOO.

🎩

Formal Chapter Events

Christmas Formal, Masquerade Ball, and other signature occasions that fill the Lodge with ceremony, tradition, and celebration.

Returning Home

Alumni Gatherings

The Lodge is a place for returning brothers to reconnect with the active chapter and with one another. Alumni events at the Lodge are among the most cherished occasions on the VOO calendar.

Whether it is an annual reunion, a formal dinner, or an impromptu visit, alumni who walk through the Lodge door find a home unchanged in essentials, even as the members change. The books on the shelves, the photographs on the walls, and the traditions around the table remind alumni that their years at VOO were part of something larger than themselves.

Preservation

Caring for the Lodge is a shared responsibility across generations

🔧

Lodge Upkeep

Regular maintenance, repairs, and improvements ensure the Lodge remains safe, functional, and welcoming. From plumbing and heating to painting and furniture, upkeep is a constant priority.

📜

Archival Care

The Lodge houses photographs, documents, artifacts, and memorabilia that record VOO's history. Proper storage, digitization, and cataloguing protect these materials for future generations.

🌿

Respect for the Space

Every member and visitor is expected to treat the Lodge with respect. This means keeping it clean, honoring its history, and understanding that it is a shared home, not merely a venue.
